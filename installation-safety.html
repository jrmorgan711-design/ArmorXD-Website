Installation & Safety

CHECK BEFORE DRILLING. Inspect both sides of the mounting surface whenever possible.

Mounting responsibility

The user is responsible for selecting a suitable mounting location, installation method and hardware for the specific application. Before drilling or installing fasteners, verify clearance from wiring, fuel lines, brake lines, tanks, batteries, plumbing, structural components, moving components and other concealed systems.

Do not install where the enclosure or hardware could interfere with safe operation. Avoid excessive heat sources and unsuitable or damaged mounting surfaces.

Kinetic Forge LLC is not responsible for damage resulting from improper installation, unsuitable mounting locations, inappropriate hardware, or modification of the mounting surface. If uncertain, consult a qualified installer familiar with the equipment.

Tracking limitations

Air Armor XD is a protective mounting enclosure only. It does not guarantee theft prevention, tracking availability, location accuracy or recovery of lost or stolen property.
