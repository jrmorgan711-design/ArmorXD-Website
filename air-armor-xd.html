Built for the Real World

Protection without unnecessary bulk.

Air Armor XD surrounds the tracker with a rigid enclosure, mechanical fasteners and a dedicated sealing interface while keeping the tracker accessible for battery replacement and inspection.

01

Gasket-Sealed Design

Designed to help protect the enclosed tracker from water, mud and debris during demanding use.

02

Stainless Hardware

Mechanical fasteners create a secure, serviceable closure instead of relying on a snap-on lid.

03

Bolt-On Mounting

Integrated mounting ears allow the enclosure to be mechanically fastened to suitable mounting surfaces.

04

Precision Tracker Fit

The internal pocket is shaped around the AirTag form factor to locate the tracker inside the enclosure.

05

Low-Profile Construction

Compact geometry helps keep the tracker discreet while still surrounding it with a protective structure.

06

Serviceable Design

Open the enclosure when the AirTag battery needs replacement or when the tracker needs inspection.
